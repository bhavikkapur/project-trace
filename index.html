Why Verified Field Evidence Matters

Project Trace 2030 is a youth driven environmental initiative focused on researching, creating and influencing environmental issues through a network of young people. Starting off with environmental research projects in India, we will create a global network of young people working together to research, teach, take action in their communities and influence environmental policy. Environmental issues are often felt at a local level but have wider impacts.

Our aim is to raise awareness of local issues by systematically using observations as data for better policy making strategies through informed involvement from the local community.

Bridging the gap between global commitments and local realities.

Climate governance has fractured into detached ministerial declarations on one side, and vulnerable, unrecorded communities on the other. Project Trace equips young researchers to bridge that divide with undeniable, peer-calibrated physical data.

The Global Challenge

Environmental issues are becoming more complex. Issues such as waste management, urban pollution, biodiversity loss and climate change can impact a community but have wider impacts on the world. Young people are often not given real opportunities to make an impact despite being some of the most affected by long term environmental decisions.

The Trace Approach

We believe that progressing the environment relies on empowering individuals. We want to provide young people with the information, research and local tools they need to become active in an area they care about.

The Four Connected Pillars

An interconnected methodology moving systematically from capability building to verified field interventions and legislative policy briefs.

Continuous Evidentiary Pipeline (01 → 04)
1. LEARN

Trace Academy

Trace Academy provides young people with knowledge and understanding in the areas of Environmental Science, Sustainable Development, Research Methodology and Public Policy through learning modules, workshops and discussions.

2. INVESTIGATE

Evidence Labs

Trace Evidence Labs will attempt to understand local environmental factors via structured fieldwork. This will help to spot any trends and produce evidence based research

3. ACT

Action Network

The Trace Action Network helps young people by engaging them in community actions, environmental education and working together on issues in their community.

4. INFLUENCE

Policy & Global

Helping young people to carry out policy research and contribute to consultations. Publishing our recommendations and looking at ways to involve young people in national and international environmental forums

India Urban Environmental Audit

Designed to document environmental conditions across selected urban locations through structured field observations and systematic data-quality checks.

PRIMARY AUDIT VERTICALS & UNIFIED SCOPE

A structured observational audit of waste management and urban greenery across public spaces in Indian cities.

Project Trace’s flagship environmental research project is the India Urban Environmental Audit. This project focuses on recording particular aspects of waste management and urban green space within Indian cities. A structured observation and geographical data collection using KoboToolbox will give us a spatial dataset to better understand environmental issues, identify trends and create evidence based conversations.

Before being added to the research dataset, each submission is checked for locational accuracy and duplication.

Governance, Integrity & Guardrails

Responsible research about the environment needs to be transparent, ethical and accountable. Project Trace 2030 will ensure this is the case in our research and projects.

PRINCIPLE 01—AUTONOMY

Organisational Independence

Project Trace is an independent youth-led project. We are committed to being open about any partnerships, funding and outside work involved with our research and programme.

PRINCIPLE 02—SAFEGUARDS

Ethical Safeguarding

Contributor safety is important. All fieldwork must be carried out under appropriate supervision, consent and safety conditions. Contributors must not be in any dangerous or restricted areas. They must not do any unsafe fieldwork.

PRINCIPLE 03—INTEGRITY

Research Transparency

We will ensure that observations are accurately documented. Submissions will be thoroughly reviewed to ensure they are complete, coherent and clearly differentiate between evidence, interpretation and recommendations. Limitations of any methodology will be clearly stated in our publications and unsupported statements will not be made.

Institutional Independence Notice: Project Trace 2030 is an independent youth-led environmental project. It is not an official UN body, government body or UN accredited observer body. The research, publications and policy work produced by Project Trace 2030 is done from independent youth perspectives.
